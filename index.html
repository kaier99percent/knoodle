Quality over quantity.

Most study apps count what you've flipped. Knoodle counts what you actually know. It tracks your confidence, separates "shaky" memory from solid knowledge, and builds your progress around honest numbers.

Open Source. Real Brand.

Knoodle is being built in the open by Kaier. The core learning engine is open-source (BSD 3-Clause), while the brand and future hosted app remain private. It's a student project growing into a real platform, one feature at a time.
