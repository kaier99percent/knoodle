Studying shouldn't feel like a mobile game.

No streaks. No fake coins. No microtransactions. Knoodle is a straight-forward, open-source tool designed to help you memorize what matters, and then get out of your way.

The Knoodle Philosophy

  • 01
    Knowledge over Gamification We don't do fake points or leaderboards. We measure what you actually know, and what you're about to forget.
  • 02
    Breaks that matter Instead of doomscrolling, play our custom-built logic games. Keep your brain sharp when you step away from the books.
  • 03
    Built in the open Knoodle is developed by a student, for students. The core engine is completely open-source and free of hidden algorithms.
