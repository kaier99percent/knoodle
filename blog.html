Rebuilding Knoodle as a workspace

I originally wanted Knoodle to be a massive learning platform. I quickly realized that building a curriculum takes an insane amount of time, and I don't have the expertise to teach every subject.

So I'm changing the direction. Knoodle is now a simple workspace. It's a place to get things done and a place to take a break.

Here is what is actually inside now:

  • Workbench: A focus timer, a typing test, and a flashcard builder. You bring the data; the app provides the tool.
  • Arcade: Minesweeper, Poker, and a few other logic games. No ads, no tracking, just a quick mental reset.

No infinite feeds. No fake points. Just tools and games.

Nov 12, 2026 // Kaier

Why I added a Poker game

When you work on a computer all day, you need to step away. But opening Reddit or Twitter just sucks you into a doomscroll loop. It doesn't actually give your brain a rest.

I built After Hours Poker as a better alternative. It's Texas Hold'em against a custom AI. You have to pay attention, calculate odds, and make decisions. It's a real break, but it's an active one.

The AI isn't perfect, but it bluffs and folds like a real player. It's a fun way to kill 10 minutes without ruining your attention span.

Oct 28, 2026 // Kaier

Designing the Focus Timer

I hate Pomodoro timers that just count down with a red number. It's stressful.

For the Workbench, I'm building a timer that builds a visual puzzle on the screen as you focus. If you stop the timer, the puzzle pauses. It gives you a reason to actually stay on task.

It's currently in development. The goal is to make the timer feel like a game you win by doing your work.
